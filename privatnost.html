PRIVACY POLICY – QUIZ ESCAPE (BEKSTVO)
Last updated: 22. 11. 2026.

The game “Quiz Escape” (also listed as “Bekstvo”, “Entkommen: Quiz” and “Escapa: Quiz”) is published by Mihajlo Mirković (“we”). Contact: mihajlomm132@gmail.com

1. DATA STORED ON YOUR PHONE
Your character's name and look, coins, items, game statistics, chosen language and country (for the language). This stays on your device until you delete the game or its data.

2. LANGUAGE BY LOCATION
On start, the game asks api.country.is which country your internet connection is in. That service sees your device's IP address and does not store it for us. We only keep the country code (e.g. “DE”) on your phone. We don't use GPS or track your movements.

3. ONLINE PLAY AND LEADERBOARD (Google Firebase)
When you open online play or the leaderboard, you get an anonymous ID. We send to Google Firebase servers (EU): your character's name and look, points, wins and games played, and data about the room you play in. Your name, look and points are VISIBLE to all players on the leaderboard. Don't use your real name if you don't want to.

3a. REPORTING AND BLOCKING PLAYERS
If you report another player's name, we send their anonymous ID and name, the reason and your anonymous ID, so we can review the report and remove inappropriate names. We keep reports for at most 12 months. Players you block are remembered only on your phone.

4. GOOGLE SIGN-IN (optional)
If you sign in with Google, we store your Google ID and email address (Firebase Authentication) and your game progress (coins, items, points) so you can use it on another device.

5. ADS (Google AdMob)
The game shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads. In the EU and wherever the law requires it, we ask for your consent before showing ads; you can change it in Settings → Ad privacy. More: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. REPORTS OF MISTAKES IN QUESTIONS
If you report a mistake in a question, we send the question text, the reason you chose, your comment (if you write one), the language and an anonymous ID. Don't put personal data in the comment. We keep reports until we have checked the question, for at most 12 months.

7. STATISTICS AND CRASH REPORTS (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)
Anonymous statistics (e.g. that a game was finished and which mode was played) help us improve the game. They don't use your name or advertising ID. In Europe they are off until you turn them on; elsewhere they are on. Crash reports contain a technical description of the error, the phone model and the system version. You can change both in Settings → About.

8. PURCHASES
Payments (the “No ads” subscription, coin packs) are handled by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation of the purchase.

9. WHY AND ON WHAT BASIS
To make the game work (online play, leaderboard, saving progress) – performance of the service you ask for. Personalized ads and statistics in Europe – your consent. Crash reports, question reports and abuse prevention – legitimate interest.

10. CHILDREN AND AGE
The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. When you create your character we ask for your year of birth (stored only on your phone). If a player is under 13: online play, the leaderboard, Google sign-in, reports and statistics are turned off, we send none of their data to our servers, and we request only child-appropriate, non-personalized ads from Google. For players under 16, ads are requested as for minors (no personalized ads). If you are a parent and believe your child sent us data, email mihajlomm132@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10a. TRANSFERS OUTSIDE SERBIA AND THE EU
Google (Firebase, AdMob) may process data in the USA and other countries. Google uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for this. More: https://business.safety.google/privacy/

11. RETENTION AND DELETION
We keep server data while you use the game. You can delete everything yourself: Settings → Delete my data. This removes your leaderboard entry, cloud progress, account and the data on your phone. You can also email us at mihajlomm132@gmail.com.

12. YOUR RIGHTS
You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict processing, to data portability and to object (GDPR and the Serbian Personal Data Protection Act). You can complain to the Serbian Commissioner (www.poverenik.rs) or to the data protection authority in your country.

13. CHANGES
If we change this policy, the new date will be shown above. The rules of play are in the Terms of Use.
POLITIKA PRIVATNOSTI – BEKSTVO
Poslednja izmena: 22. 11. 2026.

Igru „Bekstvo“ (na drugim jezicima „Quiz Escape“, „Entkommen: Quiz“, „Escapa: Quiz“) objavljuje Mihajlo Mirković (u daljem tekstu: „mi“). Kontakt: mihajlomm132@gmail.com

1. ŠTA SE ČUVA NA TVOM TELEFONU
Ime i izgled lika, dukati, kupljeni predmeti, statistika igara, izabrani jezik i država (za jezik). Ovi podaci ostaju na uređaju dok ne obrišeš igru ili podatke.

2. JEZIK PO LOKACIJI
Pri pokretanju igra pita servis api.country.is u kojoj je državi tvoja internet veza. Taj servis vidi IP adresu uređaja i ne čuva je za nas. Mi čuvamo samo oznaku države (npr. „RS“), na telefonu. Ne koristimo GPS i ne pratimo kretanje.

3. ONLINE IGRA I RANG LISTA (Google Firebase)
Kad otvoriš online igru ili rang listu, dobijaš anonimni ID. Na servere Google Firebase (EU) šalju se: ime i izgled lika, poeni, broj pobeda i odigranih partija, i podaci o sobi u kojoj igraš. Ime, izgled i poeni su na rang listi VIDLJIVI svim igračima. Ne upisuj pravo ime ako to ne želiš.

3a. PRIJAVA I BLOKIRANJE IGRAČA
Ako prijaviš ime drugog igrača, šaljemo njegov anonimni ID i ime, razlog i tvoj anonimni ID, da bismo proverili prijavu i uklonili neprikladno ime. Prijave čuvamo najduže 12 meseci. Igrači koje blokiraš pamte se samo na tvom telefonu.

4. GOOGLE PRIJAVA (nije obavezna)
Ako se prijaviš Google nalogom, čuvamo tvoj Google ID i email adresu (Firebase Authentication) i tvoj napredak u igri (dukati, predmeti, poeni), da bi ga imao i na drugom uređaju.

5. REKLAME (Google AdMob)
Igra prikazuje reklame preko Google AdMob-a. Google može da koristi reklamni ID uređaja, IP adresu i podatke o uređaju za prikaz i merenje reklama. U EU i drugde gde zakon to traži, pre reklama te pitamo za saglasnost; odluku menjaš u Podešavanja → Privatnost reklama. Više: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

6. PRIJAVE GREŠAKA U PITANJIMA
Ako prijaviš grešku u pitanju, šaljemo tekst pitanja, izabrani razlog, tvoj komentar (ako ga napišeš), jezik i anonimni ID. Ne upisuj lične podatke u komentar. Prijave čuvamo dok ne proverimo pitanje, najduže 12 meseci.

7. STATISTIKA I IZVEŠTAJI O PADU (Firebase Analytics i Crashlytics)
Anonimna statistika (npr. da je partija završena, koji režim je igran) pomaže nam da poboljšamo igru. Ne koristi ime ni reklamni ID. U Evropi je isključena dok je sam ne uključiš; drugde je uključena. Izveštaji o padu sadrže tehnički opis greške, model telefona i verziju sistema. Oba menjaš u Podešavanja → O igri.

8. KUPOVINE
Plaćanje (pretplata „Bez reklama“, paketi dukata) obavlja Google Play ili App Store. Mi ne vidimo podatke o kartici ni plaćanju; dobijamo samo potvrdu da je kupovina obavljena.

9. ZAŠTO I NA KOJOJ OSNOVI
Da bi igra radila (online igra, rang lista, čuvanje napretka) – izvršenje usluge koju tražiš. Za personalizovane reklame i statistiku u Evropi – tvoja saglasnost. Za izveštaje o padu, prijave grešaka i zaštitu od zloupotrebe – legitimni interes.

10. DECA I UZRAST
Igra je namenjena igračima od 13 godina naviše. Pri pravljenju lika pitamo za godinu rođenja (čuva se samo na telefonu). Ako je igrač mlađi od 13: online igra, rang lista, Google prijava, prijave i statistika su isključeni, na naše servere ne šaljemo nikakve njegove podatke, a od Google-a tražimo samo reklame prikladne za decu, bez personalizacije. Za igrače mlađe od 16 reklame se traže kao za maloletnike (bez personalizovanih reklama). Ako si roditelj i misliš da je dete poslalo podatke, piši nam na mihajlomm132@gmail.com i obrisaćemo ih.

10a. PRENOS PODATAKA VAN SRBIJE I EU
Google (Firebase, AdMob) može da obrađuje podatke i u SAD i drugim zemljama. Google za to koristi standardne ugovorne klauzule EU i EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Više: https://business.safety.google/privacy/
