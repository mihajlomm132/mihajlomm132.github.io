Delete your data – Quiz Escape (Bekstvo)

Developer: Mihajlo Mirković

In the game

  1. Open the game → Settings (gear icon).
  2. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

This immediately deletes your leaderboard entry, your cloud progress, your account (anonymous or Google) and all game data on the phone.

Without the game

Email mihajlomm132@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and your character's name (and Google email, if you signed in). We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: name, look, points, wins, cloud progress, account. Reports of mistakes in questions and reports of player names are anonymous and are kept for at most 12 months, then deleted. Google Play purchase records are kept by Google under its own policy.

Brisanje podataka – Bekstvo

Izdavač: Mihajlo Mirković

U igri

  1. Otvori igru → Podešavanja (zupčanik).
  2. Dodirni Obriši moje podatke i potvrdi.

Odmah se brišu tvoj red na rang listi, napredak u oblaku, nalog (anonimni ili Google) i svi podaci igre na telefonu.

Bez igre

Pošalji email na mihajlomm132@gmail.com sa naslovom „Brisanje podataka“ i imenom lika (i Google email adresom, ako si se prijavio). Podatke brišemo u roku od 30 dana i javljamo emailom.

Šta se briše, a šta ostaje

Briše se: ime, izgled, poeni, pobede, napredak u oblaku, nalog. Prijave grešaka u pitanjima i prijave imena igrača su anonimne i čuvaju se najviše 12 meseci, pa se brišu. Zapise o kupovinama na Google Play-u čuva Google po svojim pravilima.
